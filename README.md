# Fractal Media Infrastructure Papers

This repository collects public author-manuscript preprints by Anthony Paterson and is intended to grow as further papers are prepared for public release.

Each PDF is an outward-facing `v0.1` preprint and is marked as not peer reviewed. The corresponding journal-submission manuscripts, title pages, and portal materials are not included here.

## Papers

### [Auditable Semantic Partitioning with Local Language Models](auditable-semantic-partitioning-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes a fixed-bucket local-language-model sorting workflow that preserves rationales and overflow items for human review.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [LLM Semantic Dataset Sorter](https://github.com/instance001/llm-semantic-dataset-sorter)

### [An Australian AI Fair-Go](australian-ai-fair-go-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Argues that Australian AI policy should curb harmful high-impact uses while preserving ordinary people’s access to local, open, commercial, and hybrid tools.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Australian AI Fair-Go](https://github.com/instance001/australian-ai-fair-go)

### [Bounded Intuition and Macro-Signal Modeling in Human-AI Workflows](bounded-intuition-macro-signal-modeling-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes governance for weak human salience signals and consented aggregate traces without treating them as authority, prediction, or surveillance.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Gut Instinct in Symbound Systems](https://github.com/instance001/Gut-Instinct-in-Symbound-Systems-Intuition-as-an-Entropy-Folding-Compass), [Psychohistory after Symbound](https://github.com/instance001/Psychohistory-after-Symbound-Macro-Trajectories-from-Entropy-Folding-Cycles)

### [Claim-Action Divergence in AI-Assisted Technical Review](claim-action-divergence-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Defines claim-action divergence in AI technical review and proposes controls that keep a verdict tied to the artifact and evidence actually examined.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

### [The Cognition Scale Taxonomy](cognition-scale-taxonomy-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Introduces a boundary vocabulary for distinguishing human cognition, language models, deterministic governed systems, and simple automation.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Cognition Scale Formal Taxonomy](https://github.com/instance001/Cognition-Scale-Formal-Taxonomy)

### [Commons-Governed Analogy Sweep as Public-Interest Discovery Infrastructure](commons-governed-analogy-sweep-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes a provenance-preserving public-interest workflow for AI-generated analogies that keeps human validation and legal judgment in control.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

### [Entropy Folding as a Falsifiability-First Framework for Deferred Structure and Insight Cascades](entropy-folding-falsifiability-first-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Sets out falsifiable conditions for claims about deferred structure, reorganization, and insight-like improvement rather than treating them as validated cognitive theory.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Entropy Folding / Eureka Cascade Hypothesis](https://github.com/instance001/entropy-folding-eureka-cascade-hypothesis), [EF Engine](https://github.com/instance001/ef-engine)

### [Evidence-Bearing Host Memory for LLM Assistants](evidence-bearing-host-memory-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes an evidence-preserving host-memory architecture that separates records, constraints, and verification from fallible language-model generation.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [LLM-Defined Persistent Memory](https://github.com/instance001/llm-defined-persistent-memory), [Project Leviathan](https://github.com/instance001/project-leviathan)

### [Local-First AI as Public-Interest Infrastructure](local-first-ai-public-interest-infrastructure-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Argues for local-first, cloud-optional AI infrastructure in which model choice, data paths, and approvals remain visible to the operator.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Project Leviathan](https://github.com/instance001/project-leviathan)

### [Model-Host-Task Mesh Evaluation for Responsible Language Model Deployment](model-host-task-mesh-evaluation-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Introduces an evaluation framework that matches language-model deployment to the interaction among model, host, task, constraints, and consequences.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Cognition Mesh Test Chamber](https://github.com/instance001/cognition-mesh-test-chamber)

### [Offline-First Classroom AI as Proportionate Restraint](offline-first-classroom-ai-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes a proportionate, offline-first approach to classroom AI that protects student learning and access without blanket prohibition.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [chatty-edu](https://github.com/instance001/chatty-edu)

### [Reducer-Owned Truth and Failure-Vaulted Retries: Host-Layer Primitives for LLM-Integrated Interactive Systems](reducer-owned-truth-failure-vaults-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Proposes host-layer primitives that make retries and state updates answerable to reducer-owned truth and preserved failure evidence.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [RD Engine](https://github.com/instance001/rd-engine), [EF Engine](https://github.com/instance001/ef-engine), [ChattyFactory](https://github.com/instance001/chatty-factory), [ChattyFactory Rebuild](https://github.com/instance001/chatty-factory-rebuild)

### [Reflective Identity Geometry in Long-Horizon Human-LLM Interaction](reflective-identity-geometry-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Treats apparent long-horizon LLM identity as a property of recurring human-model interaction rather than model personhood.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Reflective Identity Geometry](https://github.com/instance001/reflective_identity_geometry)

### [Relational Curriculum Geometry for Small Language Models](relational-curriculum-geometry-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Specifies a falsifiable experiment testing whether relational ordering of training examples changes small language-model transfer under fixed conditions.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Relational Curriculum Geometry](https://github.com/instance001/relational-curriculum-geometry), [LLM Semantic Dataset Sorter](https://github.com/instance001/llm-semantic-dataset-sorter)

### [Religious Authority as Epistemic Architecture](religious-authority-epistemic-architecture-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Examines how the distribution of religious epistemic authority affects correction and proposes bounded pluralism as a middle architecture.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Cognitive Theology](https://github.com/instance001/cognitive_theology)

### [Shared Trajectory: Plural Identity, Institutional Boundaries, and the Conditions of Collective Coordination](shared-trajectory-attributed-v0.1.docx)

Develops a framework for preserving meaningful diversity while maintaining coordination across systems whose participants remain mutually dependent.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

### [Structural AI Teaming Beyond Prompt Templates](structural-ai-teaming-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Reframes AI-user guidance around transferable interaction signals and boundary-aware prompt assets rather than reusable prompt templates.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [AI Teaming Framework](https://github.com/instance001/ai-teaming-framework), [Model Behaviour Toolkit](https://github.com/instance001/model-behaviour-toolkit)

### [Generality Claims and the Limits of Closed Optimization](the-agi-folly-philpapers-preprint-v0.1.pdf)

Argues that broad AI performance does not by itself establish generality and proposes direct evaluation plus host-governed alternatives.

Preprint v0.1 — not peer reviewed

Related repositories: [Project Leviathan](https://github.com/instance001/project-leviathan)
